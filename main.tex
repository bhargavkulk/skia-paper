\section*{Data-Availability Statement}
We will be submitting two artifacts.
The first being the Lean mechanization of
  \muskia semantics and the rewrites discussed in \Cref{sec:rewrites}.
This Lean mechanization will also include the translation validator discussed in
  \Cref{subsec:rq1}.
The second being our fork of Skia with our optimizer
  that implements the aforementioned rewrites.
We will also be providing the script we used to pull \texttt{skp} files for
  websites, along with the original 100 websites we base msot of our evaluation on
  and the another 100 websites used for \Cref{subsec:rq4}.
